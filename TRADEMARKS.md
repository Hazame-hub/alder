# The Alder name and logo

The code is AGPL-3.0 ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). The name **Alder** and the Alder
logo are not: they are the Maintainer's marks (Hazame-hub, as in
[CLA.md](CLA.md)), used to say which software is the Alder project's own. The
AGPL licenses copyright, not names, and its section 7(e) lets a licensor
decline to license trademarks. This file is that.

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your work uses, runs, is based on or is compatible with Alder.
- Distribute **unmodified** Alder, source or the project's own builds and
  images, under its name.
- Use the name in articles, talks, tutorials, reviews and comparisons.
- Package an unmodified release for a distribution, keeping the name.

## What needs the Maintainer's written permission

- Calling a **modified** version, a fork, or any other product or service
  "Alder", or a name that could be confused with it ("Alder Pro", "AlderDS").
  A fork is welcome; it takes its own name.
- Using the logo on, or for, anything but the unmodified project.
- Offering a hosted or managed service under the Alder name.
- Anything that suggests the Maintainer made, endorses or supports your
  product when they do not.

Ask by opening an issue. Permission given for one use is not given for others.
