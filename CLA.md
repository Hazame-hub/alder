# Alder Individual Contributor Licence Agreement

**Version 1.0**

Thank you for contributing to Alder. This agreement exists for one reason, and
it is stated plainly rather than buried: it lets the Maintainer distribute Alder
under licences other than the AGPL, including commercial ones. Without it, every
contributor would hold a veto over that, and it could never happen.

You keep the copyright in everything you write. Nothing here transfers ownership.

<https://github.com/Hazame-hub/alder>.

> replace this definition with a legal name in a later version of this
> agreement. That change will not alter any other term, and contributions
> already accepted remain governed by the version signed at the time.

"**You**" means the individual who signs this agreement. If you are contributing
on behalf of an employer, read section 5 before signing.

"**Contribution**" means any work of authorship you intentionally submit to the
Maintainer for inclusion in Alder — code, documentation, configuration, tests,
or anything else — by pull request, patch, issue attachment, or any other means.
It does not include anything you conspicuously mark "Not a Contribution".

"**Alder**" means the software project owned by the Maintainer, including any
work derived from it.

If any of this stops being true, tell the Maintainer.

## 5. If your employer has rights in your work

Most employment contracts assign your employer the copyright in code you write,
sometimes including code written outside working hours. If that applies to you,
then before signing you must either

- obtain permission from your employer to make Contributions on these terms, or
- have your employer waive its rights in your Contributions, or
- have someone authorised to bind your employer sign this agreement on its
  behalf, naming the employer in place of "You".

If you sign and your employer later asserts rights in a Contribution, that is a
problem for the project, so please do not skip this section.

## 6. Third-party material

If you submit work that is not your own — a snippet from elsewhere, a vendored
dependency, anything under a separate licence — say so clearly in the pull
request, identify the source, and state the licence it comes under. Mark it
"Submitted on behalf of a third party" so it is not mistaken for your own work.

non-infringement.

The Maintainer is under no obligation to accept, merge, or keep any
Contribution.

It has not been reviewed by a lawyer on your behalf or on the Maintainer's. If
you are signing on behalf of a company, or the answer matters to you, get your

## How to sign

Open a pull request. A bot will comment on it with the exact sentence to reply
with. Your signature is recorded against your GitHub account in the
[`cla-signatures`](https://github.com/Hazame-hub/alder/tree/cla-signatures)
branch of this repository, and you only ever do it once.
